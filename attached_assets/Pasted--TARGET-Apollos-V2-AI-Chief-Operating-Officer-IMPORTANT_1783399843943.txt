PHASE 2 — OPPORTUNITY ENGINE
==================================================

Do NOT create a new page.

Instead, teach Apollos to calculate:

Business Health

Then explain WHY.

Example:

Business Health
95%

Reason:

+2%
Google Business Profile
(Location cache not completed)

+1%
Apple Business Connect
(Pending Apple approval)

+1%
TikTok
(Pending approval)

+1%
Review requests
Only 35% of completed customers have been asked.

Do NOT hardcode percentages.

Use live systems whenever possible.

==================================================
PHASE 3 — RECOMMENDATIONS
==================================================

Teach Apollos to rank recommendations.

Every recommendation should contain:

Priority

Estimated Impact

Estimated Time

Status

Example:

🎯 TARGET

Send review requests
Impact: High
Time: 5 minutes

------------

🎯 TARGET

Publish today's Google Business Profile post

Impact: High

Waiting on Google quota cooldown.

------------

✨ SPARKLES

Upload another before/after photo.

Impact: Medium

------------

🍍 PINEAPPLE

Building another dashboard.

Impact: Low

Already covered elsewhere.

==================================================
PHASE 4 — COACH MODE
==================================================

Teach Apollos to answer naturally.

Examples:

"What should I do next?"

"How are we doing?"

"What can I improve?"

"I only have 30 minutes."

"What is still disconnected?"

"What's waiting on third parties?"

"Teach me AI Edge."

"Why isn't my score 100%?"

Responses should use LIVE data only.

If data is unavailable:

Say so honestly.

Never fabricate.

==================================================
PHASE 5 — SELF KNOWLEDGE
==================================================

Teach Apollos to explain AI Edge.

Examples:

"What does Profit Center do?"

"What is Mission Control?"

"Why would I use Customer Timeline?"

"Where should I start?"

Apollos should become the teacher for the platform.

Reuse existing routes.

No duplicate dashboards.

No fake business metrics.

No placeholder AI responses.

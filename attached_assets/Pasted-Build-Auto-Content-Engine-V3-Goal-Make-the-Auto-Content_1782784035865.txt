Build Auto Content Engine V3.

Goal:
Make the Auto Content Engine smarter, more useful, and closer to a true AI marketing strategist.

Add these upgrades:

1. Content Score
   For every generated post, calculate a 1–100 score based on:

* city included
* topic included
* clear CTA
* platform fit
* caption length
* angle quality
* duplicate risk

Show score in AI Queue Inspector.

2. Best Platform Recommendation
   For each post, recommend:

* Facebook
* Google Business
* Facebook + Google

Rules:

* Google = short local presence post
* Facebook = fuller educational/promotional post
* Facebook + Google = best default for local service posts

3. Image Recommendation
   For each generated post, create an image recommendation:

* suggested image type
* suggested visual theme
* suggested text overlay

Example:
Topic: Roaches
City: Daphne
Angle: Warning

Image recommendation:
“Use roach-focused pest control image with bold warning headline, Daphne AL city text, Bed Bugs & Beyond logo, and Call Now CTA.”

4. Duplicate Protection
   Add duplicate risk detection:

* same city/topic used recently
* same angle repeated too often
* same platform combo repeated too often

Show:

* Low risk
* Medium risk
* High risk

5. AI Queue Inspector upgrades
   Add columns:

* Score
* Best Platform
* Duplicate Risk
* Image Recommendation

Expandable row should show:

* Facebook caption
* Google caption
* image recommendation
* why this post was generated

6. Smart Suggestions Panel
   Add a panel at top/right:
   “AI Suggestions”

Examples:

* “Google quota is active, schedule Facebook-only until cooldown ends.”
* “Roaches and ants are repeating often — add mosquitoes or spiders.”
* “Best next post: Fairhope Mosquitoes, seasonal angle, Facebook + Google.”
* “Queue has enough posts for 14 days.”

7. Diagnostics integration
   System Diagnostics AI Engine section should show:

* average content score
* duplicate risk count
* best next post
* queue quality status

Goal:
Auto Content Engine should feel like an AI strategist, not just a scheduler.

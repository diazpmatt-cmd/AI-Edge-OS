3. Client Repository or Resolver

The exact name may follow existing conventions.

- Build a ClientContentContext

- Avoid silently falling back to BB&B for an unknown tenant
- Preserve explicit null fallback only where legacy BB&B behavior is intentionally required

Critical tenant safety rule:

An unknown authenticated tenant must never receive the BB&B context.

BED BUG TREATMENT DIFFERENTIATOR

Bed Bugs & Beyond does not provide expensive whole-home bed bug heat treatment.

The company treats affected furniture, items, and areas individually using its targeted treatment process.

Allowed positioning:

- targeted treatment approach
- treatment of affected furniture and areas
- often more affordable than whole-home heat treatment
- avoids automatically requiring expensive whole-home heat treatment

Do not claim:

- heat treatment is always ineffective
- BB&B’s method is universally better
- guaranteed elimination
- guaranteed savings
- unsupported medical, safety, or scientific claims

TERMITE RULE

Termite service is not currently offered.

Model termites as:

- status: coming_soon
- generationAllowed: false
- bookingAllowed: false
- publishAllowed: false
- ctaAllowed: false

Do not create promotional termite content.

Do not let users manually add termites as an active topic through the UI.

FUMIGATION RULE

Fumigation is an active service and may be included in content generation.

Do not confuse fumigation with whole-home bed bug heat treatment.

WILDLIFE RULE

Wildlife removal is not currently confirmed or approved.

Model wildlife removal as disabled unless Matthew explicitly enables it later.

RECURRING PLAN RULE

Do not advertise a specific quarterly subscription, contract, or recurring residential plan unless the existing business data confirms that exact offering.

Residential Pest Control may be active without inventing plan frequency or pricing.

MOLE RULE

Keep Moles active but assign:

- low priority
- low content frequency
- no more than occasional campaigns

CAMPAIGN MIX

PHASE 9 — APPROVAL MODES

Add the missing mode:

approval_required

Supported modes:

1. draft_only
2. approval_required
3. auto_schedule

Behavior:

draft_only
- generate drafts
- no approval queue
- no automatic scheduling

approval_required
- generate drafts
- place them in a review queue
- require explicit approval before scheduling
- record approver and approval timestamp

auto_schedule
- generate and schedule automatically
- available only when explicitly enabled
- never the default for BB&B during the pilot

Migrate BB&B’s current default from auto_schedule to approval_required.

Do not change other tenants blindly.
